### Expected Final Result

- A supplier invoice document number is generated by SAP
- The invoice is posted but with status "Blocked for Payment"
- Multiple blocking reasons are recorded: "Quantity variance" AND "Price variance"
- The invoice does NOT appear in payment run selection until both blocks are resolved
- The invoice is visible in "Manage Blocked Invoices" worklist showing both blocking reasons

---

### Post-conditions

- Invoice exists with generated document number
- Invoice status is "Blocked for Payment" with multiple blocking indicators
- Both quantity and price tolerance violations are recorded
- Invoice requires resolution of both variances before payment release
