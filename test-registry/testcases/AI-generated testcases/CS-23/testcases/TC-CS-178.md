### Pre-conditions

| # | Pre-condition | Detail |
|---|--------------|--------|
| P1 | User Role | User has the role of Material Planner with SAP authorization to view and convert Purchase Requisitions to Purchase Orders |
| P2 | System Access | SAP Fiori Launchpad is accessible at `{ENV.SAP_FIORI_URL}`; valid SAP credentials are available |
| P3 | Master Data | GMP Material `{TD.materialIdConvertPR}` exists, is active, has batch management enabled, and is configured for external procurement |
| P4 | Configuration | Plant `{TD.plantConvertPR}` is configured for MRP and has a valid purchasing info record for Material `{TD.materialIdConvertPR}` |
| P5 | MRP Data | A Purchase Requisition (BA) exists for Material `{TD.materialIdConvertPR}` in Plant `{TD.plantConvertPR}` generated by a prior MRP run (HP-03 prerequisite completed) |
| P6 | MRP Data | The Purchase Requisition reflects shelf-life constraints and has a valid delivery date |

### Expected Final Result

- The planner successfully accesses the Purchase Requisition from the Stock/Requirements List
- The Purchase Requisition is reviewed and converted to a Purchase Order
- The conversion is confirmed by the system with a success message and a Purchase Order number
- The Purchase Requisition status is updated to reflect conversion (e.g., "Assigned" or "Released")
- The Purchase Order appears in the Stock/Requirements List as a confirmed receipt element
- Shelf-life constraints originally applied to the Purchase Requisition are preserved in the Purchase Order delivery date

### Post-conditions

- Purchase Requisition `{TD.purchaseRequisitionNumber}` has been converted to a Purchase Order
- The Purchase Order exists in the system with a valid PO number
- The Stock/Requirements List reflects the updated status (PR assigned/released, PO visible)
- No application-level errors or critical warnings remain outstanding
