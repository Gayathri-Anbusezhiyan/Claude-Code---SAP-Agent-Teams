## Description

As a Credit Controller, I want the system to block a Sales Order when the customer exceeds their credit limit, so that we minimize the risk of bad debt and enforce credit control policies before shipping pharmaceutical products.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The user can create a Sales Order for a customer with a defined credit limit.
2. The system performs an automatic credit check when the Sales Order is saved or released.
3. The system calculates the customer's total credit exposure (open sales orders + open deliveries + open invoices) and compares it to the credit limit.
4. The system blocks the Sales Order with a credit block status if the credit exposure exceeds the credit limit.
5. The system displays a clear error message indicating the credit limit violation and shows the current credit exposure and limit.
6. The system sends a notification to the Credit Control team when a Sales Order is blocked due to credit limit violation, allowing manual review and approval.
