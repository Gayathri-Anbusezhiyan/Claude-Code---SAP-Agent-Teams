## Description

As a Pricing Analyst, I want to maintain tiered Pricing Conditions for a pharmaceutical product based on customer group and order volume, so that contracted and list pricing is applied correctly and consistently at order entry.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The user can access the "Manage Pricing Conditions" Fiori app.
2. The user can define price scales based on order quantity breakpoints.
3. The system automatically applies the correct price tier based on the customer's pricing group and the ordered quantity.
4. The system displays the applicable condition record and its validity period on the sales order pricing screen.
5. Manual price overrides beyond a configured tolerance require supervisor approval.
6. The system flags condition records nearing their validity end date for renewal review.
