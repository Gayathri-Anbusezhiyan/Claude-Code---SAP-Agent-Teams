## Description

As a Quality Assurance Specialist, I want to link a Customer Complaint captured in Sales to a formal Quality Notification, so that product quality issues raised by customers are investigated using the same GMP-compliant process as internally identified deviations.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The user can access the "Create Quality Notification" Fiori app, initiated from the sales/return order.
2. The system pre-populates the notification with the customer, material, batch, and complaint details from the originating document.
3. The notification routes to the QA team's worklist for investigation and root-cause analysis.
4. The system tracks the notification status (Open, Under Investigation, Closed) and displays it back on the originating sales document.
5. The system prevents closure of the notification until a documented disposition and corrective action are recorded.
6. A report is available showing complaint volume and trends by material and batch.
