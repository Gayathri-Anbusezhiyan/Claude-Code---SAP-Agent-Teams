## Description

As a Maintenance Planner, I want to maintain a Resource/Work Center Master for validated production equipment, so that Process Orders can only be scheduled on equipment that is currently within its qualification and calibration validity period.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The user can access the "Manage Work Centers" Fiori app.
2. The system captures the equipment's qualification/calibration expiration date on the work center master.
3. The system prevents scheduling a process order operation on a work center whose calibration has expired.
4. The system generates an alert to Maintenance a configurable number of days before a work center's calibration is due to expire.
5. Equipment taken out of service for maintenance is automatically excluded from capacity planning until requalified.
6. The batch record references the specific equipment/work center used, including its calibration status at time of use.
